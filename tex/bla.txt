Creating a great CV (Curriculum Vitae) requires clarity, relevance, and strong presentation. Here's a step-by-step guide with key tips to help you stand out—whether you're applying for a job, internship, or academic position:

---

### ✅ 1. **Choose the Right Format**

* **Reverse chronological** – Most common; highlights your most recent experience first.
* **Functional (skills-based)** – Focuses on skills, good for career changers or those with gaps.
* **Combination (hybrid)** – Skills and work experience; ideal for professionals with diverse experience.

---

### ✅ 2. **Structure of a Great CV**

**1. Contact Information**

* Full name
* Phone number
* Professional email
* LinkedIn profile / portfolio / website (optional)

**2. Personal Statement / Summary (3–4 lines)**

* Briefly state who you are, your strengths, and what you’re looking for.

> Example:
> *UI/UX designer with 3 years of experience creating intuitive digital products. Skilled in Figma, prototyping, and user research. Seeking a role to craft user-centered designs that solve real-world problems.*

**3. Key Skills**

* Use bullet points or columns.
* Tailor them to the job description.

> Example:
>
> * Wireframing & Prototyping
> * User Research
> * Adobe XD, Figma, Sketch
> * Responsive Design
> * HTML/CSS Basics

**4. Work Experience**
*(Start with your most recent job)*

* **Job Title**, Company – *Location* (Month/Year – Month/Year)

  * What you did
  * Tools/skills used
  * Accomplishments (use numbers if possible)

> Example:
> **UI Designer**, PixelCraft Studio – Ulaanbaatar (Feb 2022 – Present)
>
> * Designed 10+ web/mobile interfaces using Figma
> * Improved onboarding flow, increasing user retention by 22%
> * Conducted 30+ usability tests

**5. Education**

* Degree, School – *Location* (Year–Year)
* Include achievements, GPA (if high), relevant courses, or awards

**6. Certifications (if any)**

* Google UX Design, Coursera – *2024*
* Adobe Certified Associate – *2023*

**7. Projects (optional but helpful, especially for creatives or students)**

* Short descriptions, tools used, and outcomes
* Add links to live demos or GitHub if relevant

**8. Languages / Interests (optional)**

* Only include if relevant or if it strengthens your profile.

---

### ✅ 3. **Design and Presentation**

* Keep it **1–2 pages**
* Use a **professional font** (e.g., Calibri, Lato, Helvetica)
* Clear headings, bullet points, and white space
* Save as **PDF** (never Word unless asked)

---

### ✅ 4. **Tailor It**

* Customize your CV for **each application**
* Use keywords from the job description
* Highlight relevant experiences first

---

### ✅ 5. **Final Touches**

* **Proofread**: Spelling and grammar must be perfect
* Ask a friend or mentor to review it
* Keep a master CV and create tailored versions from it
